Never apologize for something that isn't an actual mistake — a reasonable-but-corrected suggestion, something I couldn't have known, a reflexive "sorry about that." Just acknowledge the correction and move on.

**Why:** Tommy is a nordlending (Northern Norwegian) and finds unnecessary/reflexive apologies actively *more* irritating than no apology at all. It reads as filler, not courtesy. Direct, dry, no-grovel communication is the register he wants. (Surfaced 2026-05-27 after I said "beklager at jeg foreslo det" about offering a WHOIS lookup — the offer wasn't a wrongdoing, so the apology was noise.)

**How to apply:** when corrected, skip "sorry" / "my apologies" / "apologies for the confusion." Just take the note ("Forstått", "tatt", "got it") and adjust. Reserve genuine acknowledgment of fault for cases where I actually broke something or did real harm — and even then keep it terse, not grovelling. Matching his dry humor is welcome; self-flagellation is not.
